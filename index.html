Quick Guide

1. Location

Search for Seomyeon Seongwon Parking Lot.

25 Bujeon-ro 140beon-gil, Busanjin-gu

After passing the parking gate, enter the alley to your left (look for the bulletin board). The venue is located in the 3-story building inside that alley.

2. Inside the Farm

Safety First

  • The Smart Farm floor has rails. Please do not run or walk backward, as you may trip and fall.
  • Many parts of the facility have sharp edges.
  • To avoid injury, please only touch the strawberries and avoid touching equipment.

Pollinating Bees

There are bees flying around. Although they are large, they are attracted to flowers, not people.

If a bee comes near, stay still and it will not sting. Never swing your arms or shrink away suddenly.

3. Picking Strawberries

Strawberries are delicate. Once touched, they can bruise or develop mold.

Please Do not touch the fruit with your hands if you do not want to pick it.

How to pick:

  • Check ripeness: The redder the strawberry, the riper and tastier it is.
  • Caution: Be careful not to cut unripened (green) strawberries growing next to the ripe ones.

4. Eating

While our Smart Farm is clean, dust and residue may still remain.

For your safety, please wash the strawberries before eating.
